# IRS Lite 1.0.6 — WoW Forever

Give IRS Lite a coordinated new look with **WoW Forever**, available in **Settings > Profiles**.

- Matches Alt Tracking Assistant's teal-and-gold styling with original map artwork, ornamental frame corners, a centered banner, and matching header icons.
- Adds rounded tables and dark backgrounds at **75% opacity**, with button backgrounds at **80% opacity**.
- Adds backgrounds across the dashboard, user manual, adjustments, and settings sections, including fonts, profiles, and import, with darker transfer cards.
- Uses light gold graph bars for clearer contrast against the teal background.
- Applies immediately and saves across characters and reloads. Your earnings history and font preferences are preserved.

All theme artwork is included; **Alt Tracking Assistant is not required**. IRS Default and ElvUI Inspired remain available.

**To enable:** Open `/irslite settings`, select **Profiles**, then **WoW Forever**.

**History correction:** Existing gold moved between a personal Guild Bank and the Warband Bank is not income. This update repairs an isolated imported character overstatement when the same excess appears in day, reset, month, and total history and has one possible character owner. Account earnings and overall adjustments are preserved. The original character ledger is saved for recovery; ambiguous history is left unchanged. The repair runs automatically on reload and after importing IRS records.

**Installation:** Extract `IRS-Lite-1.0.6.zip` into your Retail `Interface/AddOns` folder, replacing the existing `IRS Lite` folder, then reload the UI. For a first-time installation, fully restart WoW.
